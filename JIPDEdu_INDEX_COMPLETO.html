About the Journal

The Journal of Inclusive Pedagogy and Digital Education (JIPDEdu) is an international, peer-reviewed, open-access academic journal devoted to research at the intersection of inclusive pedagogy, special education, educational technology, digital innovation and interdisciplinary educational studies.

The journal promotes theoretically grounded, methodologically rigorous and practice-oriented scholarship capable of contributing to accessible, equitable and high-quality educational environments.

Editorial independence

Any manuscript authored by the Founder, an editor, a board member or a closely associated person must be handled by an independent editor. The author concerned may not select reviewers, access confidential reviewer information, participate in editorial deliberations or influence the final decision.

Manuscript structure

Research articles should normally include Title, Abstract (150–250 words), 4–7 Keywords, Introduction, Theoretical/Conceptual Background, Methods, Results, Discussion, Implications for Practice, Limitations, Conclusions, Ethical Statement, Conflict of Interest Statement, Funding Statement, Data Availability Statement and References.

Reference style: APA, 7th edition.

Submission rule: manuscripts must be original and not simultaneously under consideration elsewhere.

Peer Review Policy

Research manuscripts that pass initial editorial screening will normally undergo double-blind peer review by at least two independent reviewers.

Review criteria

  • Originality and scientific relevance
  • Theoretical grounding
  • Methodological quality
  • Appropriateness of analysis
  • Consistency between results and conclusions
  • Educational relevance
  • Ethical integrity
  • Quality of references
  • Clarity and organization

Publication Ethics and Research Integrity

  • Originality and proper attribution
  • No plagiarism, fabrication or falsification
  • No duplicate or redundant publication
  • Genuine authorship and contributor responsibility
  • Conflict-of-interest disclosure
  • Accurate funding disclosure
  • Protection of human participants and vulnerable populations
  • Privacy and data protection
  • Responsible citation practices
  • Independent and confidential peer review

Artificial intelligence

AI systems may not be listed as authors. Authors remain responsible for accuracy, originality, references, interpretations and ethical compliance. Material use of generative AI should be disclosed transparently. Confidential manuscripts must not be uploaded into unauthorized public AI systems by reviewers or editors.

Corrections and retractions

Substantive post-publication errors may lead to a correction, expression of concern or retraction. Such notices should remain permanently linked to the scholarly record.
